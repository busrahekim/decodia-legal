Decodia — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Decodia (the "Game") handles information. The Game is developed and published by NoxTri ("we", "us"). It applies to the Game on iOS, Android and the web.

In short: Decodia does not ask for an account and does not collect, sell or share your personal data. Your progress stays on your device.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The Game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no in-app purchases. The mobile apps contain no advertising, analytics or tracking software, and they do not send your data to us or to anyone else.

2. Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the Game saves the following only on your device (or, for the web version, in your browser's local storage):

  • mission progress, best ranks, best times and hints used;
  • your settings (sound and vibration).

This data never leaves your device and we cannot access it. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing your browser's site data.

3. Device permissions

The mobile apps use vibration (for haptic feedback) and audio playback. They do not access your microphone, camera, contacts, location, photos or files.

4. Web version and third-party platforms

The web version may be offered on third-party web game platforms. Those platforms may show advertising and may process data under their own privacy policies and terms, which we encourage you to read. We do not receive personal information about you from them.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13. Because we do not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have questions about your rights under laws such as the GDPR or KVKK, contact us at the address below.

7. Changes to this policy

If the Game changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, if advertising is added to the mobile apps), we will update this page and the "Last updated" date before that change is released.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: support@logventure.net

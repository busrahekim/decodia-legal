Decodia — Support

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Need help with Decodia, found a bug, or have an idea? Email us at support@logventure.net. Please include your device model, operating system version and the mission you were playing.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know how to code or hack?
No. The hacking is just the story; every puzzle can be solved with logic alone, and the clues you need are always shown in the Game.

I'm stuck on a puzzle.
Tap HINT for up to three hints per puzzle. Collected documents are kept in the EVIDENCE panel, so you never have to remember them.

Where is my progress saved? Can I move it to a new phone?
Progress is saved only on your device. It is not backed up online, so uninstalling the app or switching devices starts a new game.

How do I unlock the next mission?
Missions unlock in order: complete a mission to open the next one.

What do the ranks mean?
BREACHED: mission completed. CLEAN: completed without hints. GHOST: no hints, no trace and under the target time.

Does the Game collect my data?
No. See the Privacy Policy.
